# Code of conduct

Factory welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds, identities, experience levels,
and perspectives. Participation should make the project more rigorous, useful, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical disagreement while remaining respectful toward people.
- Critique claims with evidence and give others room to revise their work.
- Assume good intent, but take reported impact seriously.
- Respect privacy, credentials, unpublished vulnerabilities, and repository boundaries.
- Help make discussions accessible to newcomers without lowering the engineering bar.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing,
credential exposure, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable. Neither is using project
infrastructure to test destructive or billable behavior without authorization.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary moderation, contact a maintainer privately through the contact information on their
GitHub profile. Do not force a person to disclose sensitive conduct publicly. Report security issues
through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through a public conduct report.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, issue a private warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban an account. Enforcement considers severity,
pattern, risk to others, acknowledgement, and repair. Maintainers must avoid conflicts of interest;
a report involving a maintainer should be handled by another maintainer when possible.

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project-controlled chat
spaces, and private project communication.
