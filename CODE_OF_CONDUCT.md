# Code of conduct

Factory contributors and users deserve a respectful, productive space. In issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project discussions, be considerate of different experience levels and perspectives. Critique ideas and code without attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and sharing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and limit participation when needed to protect the community. They will consider context and apply a response proportionate to the behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting channel](https://github.com/clockgrove/factory/security/advisories/new) and mark the report as a conduct concern. Please do not post sensitive details in a public issue. A maintainer will acknowledge the report, review it discreetly, and explain any action they can share while protecting the people involved.
