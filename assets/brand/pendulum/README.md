# Clockgrove Pendulum logo package

The mark is the lower half of a grandfather clock's case: an arched wooden window, and inside it a brass pendulum caught mid-swing, whose bob is a cut log slice with its tree rings, with one leaf at the arch. It belongs to the Clockgrove family (the Living Rings) in palette and painted style.

## Master files

- `master/pendulum-mark-original.png`: the approved artwork with its transparency, untouched.
- `master/pendulum-mark-transparent-1024.png`: the mark alone on transparency, centred in a 1024 px square.
- `master/pendulum-icon-cream-1024.png`: the primary icon on warm cream.
- `master/pendulum-icon-slate-1024.png`: full-bleed square icon on slate, for platforms that apply their own corner mask.

The plugin's listing icon (`plugin/assets/icon.png`) and `docs/img/icon.png` are the cream icon; `docs/img/social-preview.png` is the repository's 1280×640 social preview.

## Web files

- `web/favicon.ico`: bundled 16, 32 and 48 px favicon.
- `web/favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-48.png`: the mark alone, at each size.
- `web/apple-touch-icon.png`: 180 px, on cream.
- `web/icon-192.png`, `web/icon-512.png`: web-app manifest sizes, on cream.

## Usage notes

- Use the cream icon as the primary app, listing and avatar treatment; slate where a dark surface needs more contrast.
- Use the transparent mark over quiet cream, off-white or slate surfaces.
- Keep the painted texture: do not trace the mark into flat vector art, and do not add clock numerals, robot or AI motifs, or glossy effects.
- `preview/pendulum-logo-preview.png` shows the mark, both icons and the small sizes together.
