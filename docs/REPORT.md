# The cost report

`pendulum report` reads what Pendulum's own records say about a period or a set of Objectives and prints every figure already computed: as JSON with `--json` (the schema below), else as plain Markdown for a terminal or CI. Pendulum is only the data layer. Your coding agent presents the JSON however you ask (a table, a chart, an HTML page, a document) through the [`/pendulum:pendulum-report`](../plugin/skills/pendulum-report/SKILL.md) skill, and it never adds, averages or derives a dollar figure itself.

```sh
pendulum report [--since DATE] [--until DATE] [--issue N[,N...] | --label NAME | --parent N | --compare A,B | --compare-label A --compare-label B] [--json] [--max-calls N] [--by account] [--rates MODEL[,MODEL...]] [--app ID] [--self LOGIN]... [--repo OWNER/NAME]
```

- **Scope.** `--since` and `--until` (a date, or a time with its zone; `--until` is exclusive) give a period: every Objective with a stage that started in it. `--issue`, `--label` and `--parent` (the parent issue's sub-issues) give a set of Objectives, read whole unless a period is given too. `--compare` and `--compare-label` give two sets, the arms of an experiment, side by side (Compare two arms, below). A period with no `--since` (and no set) starts 30 days before `--until`, or before now, at that day's UTC midnight (`scope.defaultPeriod`). A decision-spend line, which records one UTC day's decision calls, counts when that day's start (its midnight) falls in the period.
- **Reads.** The issue comments hold every marker the report needs: the progress, harness, spend, ledger, vendor, decision-spend and finish markers, and a review stage's findings record (below). For `--issue`, each named issue's own comments are read; otherwise one paged repository-wide read (`issues/comments?since=`). GitHub filters that read by when each comment was last updated, so for each Objective with a record in it whose issue was opened before the period's start, the report also reads the Objective's own thread, as `--issue` does, up to its first comment written since the start (every comment the repository-wide read lacks was written before it), each comment kept once: every figure comes from all of the Objective's records, so a period's figures, its review findings and sandbox included, add up with the next period's to those of both together. The Objectives' issues and the PRs on their `pendulum/<n>` branches give each one's outcome (for `--issue`, each branch's own PRs). A period's PR list is read newest updated first and stops at its first page older than the period's start (or the set's oldest issue), so it holds every PR updated since then; a closed Objective whose issue is older than that, with no PR of its own branch created since then in the list, has its branch's own PRs read too. A merged Objective is read from its finish card's summary alone (below), its stage comments and usage artifacts unread, when the card is Pendulum's own, every stage of Pendulum's in it has its per-pass figures, its run (its clock to its merge) lies in the period, every stage comment of the Objective in the period is one the card counted, and no stage comment of the Objective, Pendulum's or one by someone who can push, was written or edited after the card (a late vendor booking, a changed spend line); its figures are then the same its stages give, and its review findings still come from its review stages' findings records, each stage's dollars from the card. With `--by account` or `--rates`, whose figures no summary carries (each stage's account; a Codex stage's sub-agent threads and long-context share), no Objective is read from its summary: its stages are read. `--by account` reads every stage instead: a stage's account is in its own records only. A stage's per-pass ledger, tokens, Pendulum release and (when its harness line names none, as Copilot's at its default model) model come from the ledger line beside its spend line (below), with no artifact read, even where its usage record is read for another stage of the Objective, as its finish card takes them: a stage's figures are the same whichever path reads them. A stage's model is the first of its own harness line's, its ledger line's and its usage record's that names one, as the finish card names it; never another stage's, and null (`no-model`) when none does. Usage artifacts are listed and read only for the stages whose comment does not say everything: a stage with no spend line, for its dollars; a stage with no ledger line (a stage from before it, or one whose ledger did not fit) whose ledger no local record in this clone holds, for those figures; and a stage whose harness line and ledger line name no model, for its model, unless a local record in this clone names it. A stage with a spend line keeps that line's dollars, so none is counted twice: its ledger, from its comment, else its usage record or local mode's own record of it in this clone, gives its passes and tokens, never its total. A usage record that names no stage comment, beside a comment of its stage with dollars (cost-join's `unmatched`), may be that stage: its dollars are unknown, and none of its passes or tokens is counted. An artifact read with no `result.json` in it (its stage ended before writing one) leaves its stage one run of unknown cost, `unreported`. A cloud sandbox stage that left no progress comment counts the model dollars its vendor comment carries (`modelUsd`); one with none, and no model run of unknown cost, is one run of unknown cost (`unreported`), as on its finish card. The Objectives with a stage that has no spend line are read first, so a call budget that runs out costs per-pass detail before a dollar figure. A record that names a stage comment the report does not attribute to Pendulum is that stage's: it adds no stage and no dollars. Artifacts uploaded before the period began are not read. An artifact a fork's run uploaded (its workflow run's head repository is not the repository), or one that names no repository id, is never read, whatever its name: it is counted as `artifact-foreign`. Each artifact read is downloaded with `gh run download`, which reads its run's artifact list, and kept by its artifact id under your cache directory (`$XDG_CACHE_HOME`, else `~/.cache`; `~/Library/Caches` on macOS), in `pendulum/artifacts/<API host>/<owner>/<name>/`, with only the fields the report reads, never in the repository, so a later report downloads it no more. Local mode's own records under `<clone>/.pendulum/<n>/` are read for the per-pass ledger when the command runs in the clone. No job or list of workflow runs is read, and no timeline but those a labelled set needs (Who applied the label, below); nothing is written.
- **Budget.** The command prints its planned GitHub API calls to stderr before it reads: a paged read's first page, then, from that page's `Link` header, the pages left (said as "at least" where GitHub names no last page), until a page whose `Link` header names no next page (a full first page with no `Link` header at all ends the read too, and `no-link` says so); a read that can end early (the PR list, newest first, at the first PR older than the comments read; an Objective's own thread at its first comment written since the period's start) plans each further page as it is reached; for `--issue`, each issue's comment pages from its comment count; each Objective's own thread from before the period's start, its first page; for a label whose applier is checked, each listed issue's timeline and, as each one is first met, a labeller's permission; after an Objective's artifact lists, the downloads its artifacts not yet cached take, two each. A merged Objective read from its summary plans no artifact read. It stops at `--max-calls` (300 by default). A stopped report still prints what was read, with `source.complete` false, and exits 3: an Objective whose PRs were not read (the list, or its own branch's) has no outcome (null) unless a state label or an open issue decides it, and a stage whose usage record was not reached is of unknown cost for `stopped`. An Objective whose comments were not all read (`comments-stopped`) is printed from those that were: the comments after them, when the repository-wide read or its own was cut short, are one run of unknown cost for `stopped`, and so is each vendor comment whose stage's progress comment was not read (it may book for a stage before the period).
- **Token.** Local mode's token for the repository when it is saved, else your `gh` login: the report shows only what that login can read.
- **Trust.** A marker counts only from Pendulum's own login (the token's), a login `--self` names (each one, exactly, and only on a comment by someone who can push), or Pendulum's App. The App's numeric id comes from `--app`, else the repository variable `PENDULUM_APP_ID` (one call; `pendulum setup` writes it beside the secret of that name; when GitHub answers the token 403 or 404, which a token without Variables: read may get, one more call with your `gh` login's token, never printed; that read failing, or refused, never ends the report), else its local copy beside the local token (`~/.config/pendulum/repos/OWNER/NAME.json`, which setup, `serve` and doctor save: LOCAL.md, Set it up), else `PENDULUM_LOCAL_APP_ID`. No name or slug ever stands in for it. With none, `source.missing` says why, whether or not a comment through an App was read: `app-unreadable` (no token may read the variable) or `app-not-given` (it is not set). A stage comment by anyone else who can push, or through another GitHub App, is a stage of unknown cost (`unattributed`), never its figures; its vendor comment adds nothing to it, one with no progress comment is a run of unknown cost but no stage, and its decision-spend lines are decision calls of unknown cost. Anyone else's, `github-actions[bot]` included, counts nothing. A finish card's summary counts only on Pendulum's own comment, as every marker does; anyone else's card is no summary, and the Objective's stages are read. `source.trusted` lists the logins and the App the report trusted. For an install from before the variable, save it once: `gh variable set PENDULUM_APP_ID --repo OWNER/NAME --body <the App's numeric id>` (doctor names the same line when it is missing, and on GitHub Actions when it names another App).

## Compare two arms

Tag the Objectives of each arm of an experiment (a model, an effort, a setting) with an experiment label, `pendulum:experiment:<name>` ([USAGE.md](USAGE.md), Run an experiment), then put the two arms side by side:

```sh
pendulum report --compare sonnet,opus          # pendulum:experiment:sonnet against pendulum:experiment:opus
pendulum report --compare-label wave-6 --compare-label wave-7   # any two labels
```

- **Arms.** `--compare` takes two experiment names (the part after `pendulum:experiment:`), joined by a comma or as the flag twice; `--compare-label` takes two label names. Each arm is every issue with its label, read whole: `--since` and `--until` are refused with them, since a period would cut a merged Objective's dollars to the stages in it. An issue both arms count for is in neither, listed in `comparison.inBoth`.
- **Who applied the label.** An arm's label counts for an issue only when the person who last applied it, by the issue's timeline, can push or is a maintainer the workflow names, as for the harness and model labels: a label applied by anyone else, by a bot or through an App is not counted (`untrusted`, missing code `label-untrusted`), nor one whose labeller's permission the token may not read (a 403: `unverifiable`, missing code `label-unverifiable`). An issue both arms list counts only once each arm's check of it ended. A `--label` that is an experiment label follows the same rule. The check reads each listed issue's timeline once, and each labeller's permission once, before any other record of it; the other labels a `--label` names are read as GitHub has them.
- **Per arm:** n (its Objectives) and their outcomes; then, over its merged Objectives, the median and p25..p75 of the dollars per merged Objective of each billing kind, the model minutes (every harness pass of each stage's per-pass ledger, from its progress comment's ledger line or its usage record; for a merged Objective read from its finish card, the summary's `modelMinutes`, as are its fix passes and its wall clock, `passes.fix` and `labelledWallMs`, the same figures), the wall clock (from the Objective's start to the merge, the scorer's rule: its first `pendulum:objective` or `pendulum:running` label, or for one filed as a draft the start that led to its first Running), the review rounds (review stages) and the fix passes; and its runs of unknown cost with their reasons. A kind's median counts every measured merged Objective, so one billed at another kind counts as $0 in it. A merged Objective with a run of unknown cost is left out of every dollar measure, and one whose measure was not recorded out of that measure (model minutes and fix passes: a stage with no ledger, or a stage comment Pendulum cannot attribute to itself; wall clock: no start event); each is counted as `leftOut`. The medians and quartiles interpolate between the closest ranks, the same computation as the bench's baseline line.
- **No claims.** The figures stand alone: no difference between the arms, no test of significance, no total across kinds or across arms. Every measure carries its n, and the Markdown shows each arm's rows under the table. The Markdown shows the comparison and the source only; the JSON's `totals`, `breakdowns`, `objectives` and `hotSpots` cover both arms together.

## Rate ratios

`--rates` names candidate models (`--rates gpt-6.1-terra,gpt-6-luna`, or the flag again; an alias and the model it names count once). For each recorded model, the report gives each candidate's published rates over that model's own, on that model's recorded token mix, as one ratio with no unit:

- **One vendor only.** A ratio is read from one vendor's published rate table, the same for both models, and two vendors' models are never compared (`other-vendor`). Pendulum holds one table, OpenAI's (the Codex price table, `plugin/scripts/lib/codex-prices.json`), whose rows are the candidates it can weigh. Anthropic's models have none, since Claude Code reports its own dollars, so a Claude model gets no ratio (`no-rate-table`), as the recorded model or as a candidate. A candidate that is the recorded model is said (`same-model`), not weighed.
- **The mix.** Each model's tokens over its stages: a stage's tokens under its model, as `breakdowns.model` counts them, except that a Codex stage's sub-agent threads (its usage record's `subAgents`) are under each thread's own model and the rest under the stage's, as the cost join prices them. A stage's decision records (its ledger's `decision` entries) are in no model's mix. A stage whose ledger's passes ran on more than one model (a review stage's review pass on the reviewer's model, its answer and fix passes on the worker's), or on another model than the one its harness line names (an alias or a dated snapshot of it aside), is split by its passes: each model gets its own passes' tokens, and a Codex sub-agent thread its record lists comes out of the passes it ran in and goes under its own model. A pass's long-context share is known from a ledger only when its whole input is under the threshold. A stage that may have run passes on a model other than its own with no ledger naming each pass's model (a review stage with no ledger, or whose ledger line does not name a pass's model) gives its model and the worker's no ratio (`pass-models`). Where no record splits the sub-agent threads out (a stage known only from its progress comment, or from another harness's record), the passes on the worker's model cannot be split from the Objective's sub-agent model when that is another: both models get no ratio (`sub-agents`); a review pass on the reviewer's model marks neither. A model with a stage that recorded no input or output tokens gets none (`no-tokens`).
- **The weighing.** As the cost join prices Codex tokens: the uncached input (input less its cache reads and writes) at the input rate, the cache reads at the cached rate, the cache writes at the write rate (the input rate for a model with none) and the output at the output rate; and the long-context share (the tokens of requests over the table's `longContextAbove`, from each stage's usage record) at each model's long-context rates. Two models with no long-context rates weigh every token at the standard rates. Otherwise a share not known (a stage whose input passed the threshold with no usage record saying how much of it came from requests over it: its requests not read, or its tokens from its progress comment alone) gives no ratio, nor does a share that is not zero where either model has no long-context rates (`long-context`). A promotional rate counts only before its end (`price-ended` after it).
- **No dollars.** A ratio is not a cost and has no dollar unit; the report holds no figure of what another model would have cost. The `/pendulum:pendulum-report` skill's proposals, written only when the user asks, cite these ratios as given.

## Rules

- Dollars are vendor-reported (Claude Code's `total_cost_usd`, Copilot's AI credits, the AI Gateway's cost, the sandbox vendor's usage at its list rates), or API-reported tokens at the published rate (the Codex price table), or unknown. Nothing is estimated, bounded or counterfactual. The figures are the scorer's own: both use one cost join (`plugin/scripts/lib/cost-join.mjs`), each stage counted once across its usage record and its spend line.
- Totals stay split by kind and are never added together: `api` (billed per use on a key), `subscription` (API-equivalent at list prices, on the subscription), `credits` (Copilot AI credits, billed to the seat), `unknown` (billing not recorded) and `sandbox` (the cloud sandbox vendor's cost). The report carries no total across kinds.
- Spend that was not reported is a count of runs of unknown cost, each with its reasons, never $0.
- Hot spots are ranked by exact counts and ratios of them; nothing is flagged against a threshold.
- No reason, Block text, task card, transcript or credential string is in the report, and no account unless `--by account` asks for it.

## Schema

The JSON's `schema` is `pendulum-report/1`. A change that renames, removes or retypes a field is a new version; a new field is not, so `comparison` and `scope.compare` are fields of `pendulum-report/1`. Dollars are US dollars, rounded to a millionth. A **cost** is `{usd: {api, subscription, credits, unknown, sandbox}, unknownRuns, unknownWhy}`: the known dollars of each kind (0 for none), the runs of unknown cost and their reason codes. **Tokens** are `{input, output, cached, written}`, summed where a record reports them (input includes the cache reads and writes), null where none does.

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `schema` | `pendulum-report/1` |
| `repository` | `OWNER/NAME` |
| `generatedAt` | When the report was made (ISO) |
| `pendulum` | The Pendulum release that made it |
| `scope` | `since`, `until` (ISO or null), `defaultPeriod`, `issues`, `label`, `parent`, `compare` (the two arms' label names, or null), `by` (the extra breakdowns asked for; with `account`, no Objective is read from its summary) |
| `words` | The words for each cost kind, to label the figures with exactly |
| `totals` | `objectives`, `stages`, `passes` (from per-pass ledgers), `cost`, `tokens` |
| `unknown` | `runs`; `stages` (the stages with runs of unknown cost: a decision record, or a vendor comment with no stage of its own, is an item but no stage); `reasons`: `{code, stages, runs, text}` per reason code, `stages` the stages (and decision records) naming it and `runs` its runs where no stage gives it with another reason, else null; `items`: `{issue, stage, runs, why}` per stage with unknown cost (`stage` null for the comments of an Objective the call budget left unread) |
| `breakdowns` | `stage` (`work`, `rebase`, `review`, `decisions`: the decision calls outside a stage; null: an Objective's comments the call budget left unread), `pass` (by pass label: the ledger label without its count, `work-2` as `work`; from the ledgers, with `modelMinutes`), `model` (one name per model: an OpenAI model the Codex price table lists by the table's id, after any provider prefix and through the table's aliases, a dated snapshot suffix kept, so `openai/gpt-6.1-sol` is `gpt-6.1-sol` and `gpt-5.6` is `gpt-5.6-sol`; a Claude model's other name that `plugin/scripts/lib/claude-models.json` lists (a GitHub Copilot catalog id) by the Anthropic API id it maps it to, so `claude-sonnet-5.5` is `claude-sonnet-5-5`; any other id as recorded), `harness`, `version` (the Pendulum release that ran the stage), and `account` with `--by account`. Each row: `key` (null where no record names it), `stages` (`passes` for `pass`), `cost`, `tokens`. Every breakdown but `pass` covers the totals |
| `findings` | The review findings and their outcomes (below): `outcomes` (the outcome codes, in order), `totals`, `byReviewer`, `byStage` |
| `objectives` | One row per Objective: `issue`, `title`, `url`, `outcome` (`blocked` or `question` by its state label; else `open` while the issue is open; else `merged` when the newest PR on `pendulum/<n>` from the repository itself, not a fork, merged, or `closed-unmerged`; null when its issue, or for a closed one its PRs, could not be read), `stages` (`work`, `rebase`, `review` counts), `passes` (`total`, `answer`, `fix`; null without a ledger for every stage), `cost`, `tokens`, `modelMinutes`, `usdPerModelMinute` (per billing kind: that kind's `usd` over the `modelMinutes` of the `passes` whose dollars were all reported, the same minutes for every kind; never summed across kinds), `unpricedPasses`, `findings` (its review findings, as `findings.totals` counts them; null with no findings record, zero counts with its records all outside the period) |
| `hotSpots` | Each `{rankBy, items}`, ranked by `rankBy`, largest first: `reviewLoops` (review stages per Objective), `answerAndFixPasses` (answer and fix passes per Objective; `unrecorded` lists the Objectives with no ledger), `cacheWriteShare` (per pass label: `passLabel`, `passes`, `written`, `input`, `share`), `costPerModelMinute` (per Objective and billing kind), `blockedOrUnmergedSpend` (the Objectives that ended Blocked or closed unmerged, with their `stages` and `cost`) |
| `comparison` | Null without `--compare` or `--compare-label`. Else `inBoth` (issues both labels count for, in neither arm) and `arms`, two, in the order given: `label`, `experiment` (its name, or null for another label), `n`, `outcomes` (a count per outcome, `unread` for null), `merged`, `usdPerMerged` (per cost kind), `modelMinutes`, `wallMinutes`, `reviewRounds`, `fixPasses`, each `{median, p25, p75, n, leftOut}` over the merged Objectives (null where none has the measure); `findings` (counts over the arm's Objectives with a findings record: `objectives`, `findings`, `outcomes`, `reviewStages`, `unnamed`; Review findings, below); `unknown` (`runs`, `objectives`, `reasons`: `{code, objectives, text}`); `untrusted`, `unverifiable` and `unchecked` (issues with the label not counted: its last applier cannot push, its applier's permission could not be read, or the call budget stopped before the check); `objectives` (per Objective: `issue`, `outcome`, `usd` per kind, `unknownRuns`, `modelMinutes`, `wallMinutes`, `reviewRounds`, `fixPasses`, `findings`: its review findings, null without a record) |
| `rates` | Null without `--rates`. Else `words` (what a ratio is, to label it with exactly), `candidates` (the models named, an alias once), `basis` (the tables and their dates the ratios read, or null with none), `tables` (`{vendor, file, date}` per published rate table), `items` (`{model, rateOf, candidate, candidateRateOf, vendor, mix, long, ratio}`: the model, as `breakdowns.model` names it, and the table row it read, the candidate and its row, their vendor, `mix` the recorded tokens and `long` their long-context share, each as `{uncached, cached, written, output}`, and `ratio` the candidate's rates over the model's on that mix, to a millionth) and `skipped` (`{model, candidate, why, text}` for each model, candidate or pair with no ratio: `no-rate-table`, `price-ended`, `other-vendor`, `same-model`, `no-tokens`, `sub-agents`, `pass-models`, `long-context`; `model` or `candidate` null where the reason is the other's alone) |
| `source` | `complete`, `stoppedAt`, `maxCalls`, `calls` (`planned`, `plannedAtLeast`, `made`, `byKind`), `trusted` (`logins`, `app`, `appFrom`: where the App id came from, `flag`, `variable`, `variable-gh` (read with the `gh` login's token after a 403), `local-copy` or `env`, or why there is none, `unset` (404 to the last token that read it, or a value that is no App id) or `forbidden` (403 to it); null when the call budget stopped before the read; `appWhy`: why the variable gave no id, `unset` or `forbidden`, also when the local copy or `PENDULUM_LOCAL_APP_ID` stands in for it, which stderr then names as unverified; null when it gave one), `read` (comments, Pendulum's own, not attributed and ignored; issues, PRs, artifacts listed, downloaded, cached, expired, failed, unreadable and from forks; local records; `summaries`, the merged Objectives read from their finish card's summary) and `missing`: `{code, count, counts, issues, text}` for each thing that was not read or not recorded. `count` is in the code's own unit: the Objectives it names, (`artifact-foreign` too, the Objectives whose artifacts came from forks), but `ledger-stopped`'s stages, `app-not-given`'s and `app-unreadable`'s comments, the artifacts for `artifact-expired`, `artifact-failed` and `artifacts-unreadable`, and 1 for `stopped` and `self-unknown`. `counts` is `{objectives, stages}`: the Objectives it names and the stages of theirs it concerns (for `unattributed`, the stage comments Pendulum cannot attribute), each null where the code concerns neither. `ledger-stopped` also lists its stages, `stages`: `{issue, stage, key}` |

`missing` codes: `stopped` (the call budget ended the reads), `stopped-before-records` (Objectives whose usage records the budget did not reach, for stages with no spend line), `comments-stopped` (Objectives whose comments the budget stopped the report before reading in full: the comments after those read, when unread, and each vendor comment whose stage's progress comment was not read are runs of unknown cost for `stopped`; their review findings may count an earlier round's again), `ledger-stopped` (stages with a spend line whose per-pass ledger the budget did not reach; its entry also lists them, `stages`: `{issue, stage, key}`), `self-unknown`, `app-unreadable` (no App id: the variable could not be read, 403), `app-not-given` (no App id: the variable is not set, or holds no App id), each counting the comments through an App or by another login that can push that were read (0 when none was), `unattributed`, `no-record` (a stage with no spend line and no usage record read), `artifact-foreign` (usage artifacts from runs of forks, or naming no repository id, never read), `no-ledger` (stages without a per-pass ledger in their comment or usage record: stages from before it, or a local stage whose ledger did not fit its comment and that has no local record on this machine), `no-model` (stages whose harness line, ledger line and usage record name no model, or that have no usage record: under no model in `breakdowns.model`), `model-stopped` (stages whose model only their usage record names, which the call budget stopped the report before reading), `no-version`, `issue-unread`, `label-untrusted` (issues whose vetted label was last applied by someone who cannot push, a bot or an App), `label-unverifiable` (issues whose label's last applier's permission the token may not read), `label-unchecked` (issues whose label's applier the call budget stopped the report before reading), `in-both-arms`, `no-link` (paged reads that ended at a full first page with no `Link` header, so whether more followed is not known: `count` the reads, `issues` those whose own thread such a read was), `artifact-expired`, `artifact-failed`, `artifacts-unreadable` (lists the token may not read, or artifacts that name no workflow run).

## The finish card's summary

Each finish card ([USAGE.md](USAGE.md#the-finish-card)) carries one hidden line under its `<!-- pendulum:<n>:finish -->` marker: `<!-- pendulum:<n>:summary {...} -->`, its JSON on one line with `<` and `>` escaped. It holds the figures the card shows and each stage's per-pass figures, from the same records the card is drawn from, never worked out another way: a stage's passes come from its ledger (the ledger line beside its spend line, else its usage record's, its times to the millisecond) through the same code the report folds a stage's ledger with. `v` is its version, `1`: a change that renames, removes or retypes a field is a new version, and a reader reads the Objective's stages for a version it does not take, or for a summary any figure of which does not read. Dollars are US dollars to a millionth, `cents` the whole cents the card shows (its rows add up to the total's), times are ISO, and `ms` and `wallMs` are milliseconds. It is read only from the line under the `finish` marker, and only from a card with at least one stage. A line over 30,000 characters is written with every stage's `passes` null; still over it, or with the comment over GitHub's 65,536 characters, the card is posted without a summary.

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `v`, `pendulum`, `issue` | The summary's version, the Pendulum release that posted the card, and the issue |
| `started`, `merged`, `wallMs` | The card's clock (the label that started the run), the merge, and the wall clock between them |
| `prs`, `commits`, `additions`, `deletions`, `files` | The merged PRs and their change, every part of a split Objective added up |
| `checks`, `reviews` | `{passed, failed, required}` of the checks that gated the merge, and `{count, findings}` of Pendulum's reviews; each null for none |
| `stageCounts` | `{work, rebase, review}` as the report counts stages: a build and a split part are `work` |
| `passes`, `modelMinutes`, `tokens` | The Objective's passes `{total, answer, fix}` (null unless every stage's are known: each of Pendulum's stages has its ledger, and none is a stage Pendulum cannot attribute), the minutes of every harness pass (null unless its passes are known and each harness pass has its times), and every stage's tokens summed |
| `labelled`, `labelledWallMs` | The scorer's start of the clock (the first `pendulum:objective` label, with no restart window) and the wall clock from it to the merge; null with no label event |
| `cost` | `{usd, cents, unknown, why, byBilling}`: the known dollars, the runs of unknown cost with their reason codes, and the dollars by billing kind; the cloud sandbox's dollars are in it, and apart below |
| `sandbox` | The cloud sandbox's own cost `{usd, cents, unknown, why, foreign}`, or null: `why` is `sandbox` for a stage's own vendor comment whose usage the vendor did not report and `unattributed` for one Pendulum cannot attribute; `foreign` the stage kind (`work`, `review`) of each vendor comment Pendulum cannot attribute of no stage with a progress comment, one each |
| `stages` | In time order: `kind` (`build`, `part`, `work`, `rebase`, `review`), `key` (its progress comment's run key, as its usage record and findings record name it), `part`, `model`, `harness`, `version` (the Pendulum release that ran it), `ms` (null for a stage with no progress comment), `usd` (null: none known), `cents`, `unknown`, `why`, `byBilling`, `sandbox` (its own vendor comment's `{usd, unknown}`, or null), `churn` (its churn rows' codes), `tokens` (`{input, output, cached, written}`, a count left out where none is reported), `passes` and `unattributed` (true for a stage Pendulum cannot attribute) |
| `stages[].passes` | Null with no ledger; else one entry per pass kind (the report's pass label, `decision` for a decision record), in the order of its first pass: `kind`, `passes` (how many), `cost` (a report cost, a kind at $0 left out), `tokens`, `minutes` (their model minutes), `harnessMinutes` (the same without decision records; left out when equal to `minutes`), `untimed` (its harness passes with no start or end), `cache` (`{passes, written, input}` over the passes reporting both), `priced` (`{minutes, passes, cost}` of the harness passes whose dollars were all reported) and `unpriced` (the other harness passes with times); `minutes`, `untimed`, `cache`, `priced` and `unpriced` are left out when there are none |
| `asked` | Where the dollars went, the work the issue asked for: `{part, what, usd, cents, unknown, byBilling}`, `what` one of `work`, `review`, `answer`, `fix`, or `sandbox` for the cloud sandbox's row |
| `churn` | The churn Pendulum caused: `{row, stages, usd, cents, unknown, byBilling}`, `row` one of `rebase-conflict`, `rebase-clean`, `catch-up`, `judge`, `block` |

## The per-pass ledger

Each stage's usage record (`result.json`: the GitHub Actions artifact `pendulum-usage-<n>[-review]`, or local mode's `<clone>/.pendulum/<n>/usage-*/`) carries `ledger`, one entry per harness pass and decision record: `label`, `decision` and `calls` for a decision record, `model`, `effort`, `started`, `ended`, `usd`, `usdUnknown`, `usdUnknownWhy`, `billing`, `usdByBilling` (its dollars by kind, a relayed key's share as `api`), `workerKeyUsd`, `tokens`, `resumed` and `firstCache` (its first request's cache split). Its `ran` names the worker's and the reviewer's effort and the Pendulum release (`pendulum`). The artifact keeps it 90 days, and the report's cache keeps what it read.

Every stage, on every route, also writes its ledger into its progress comment, on the line after its spend line, when the stage ends: `<!-- pendulum:ledger {...} -->`, numbers and labels only (no reason, Block text, prompt, path or credential). Its keys are short: `v` (the Pendulum release), `m` (the stage's model), `b` (the first pass's billing) and `p`, one entry per pass with `l` (label), `d` (a decision record's calls), `m` and `b` where the pass's differ from the stage's, `e` (effort), `t` (start and end, Unix seconds, so to the second), `u` (known dollars, to a millionth, as the spend line), `q` and `w` (runs of unknown cost and their codes), `y` (dollars by billing, where they are not all at the pass's own kind), `k` (a relayed key's share), `n` (input, output, cached and written tokens), `r` (1 when it resumed a session) and `c` (its first request's cache read and write). A model is written only when the stage's harness line already names it (its own, or the Objective's worker, sub-agent or reviewer model), so an id the harness resolved itself (a Bedrock inference profile ARN names an AWS account) never reaches the comment: a pass on another model reads as `m: null`. A stage whose harness line names no model at all (Copilot at its default) writes its own model from its usage record, a name from GitHub's model catalog. A ledger is not written when its line would pass 6,000 characters, or when it would take the comment past 60,000 (GitHub refuses one over 65,536): the comment says so in a line of its own, the marker carries the pass count alone (`{"cut": N}`), and the usage record stays that stage's ledger. The report reads the line for a stage's passes, tokens (its passes' summed), model and release, and the finish card for its model; both only on Pendulum's own comments (Trust, above), and neither for dollars: a stage's dollars are always its spend line's. A stage with no ledger line (one whose ledger did not fit, or from before the line) takes its ledger from its usage record or local record, its times to the millisecond; from the line, pass minutes are to the second, on the stage path and the finish card alike.

## Review findings

Each review stage records how every finding it handled ended, in its own progress comment's findings line (`<!-- pendulum:findings {...} -->`): `pr`, `reviews` (the ids of the reviews its own reviewer posted) and `items`, one per finding, `{id, outcome, review, from, listed}`: the finding's comment id (or `r<review>-<n>` for one in a review body), its outcome, the review that raised it (Pendulum's reviewer's), the login of the reviewer outside Pendulum that raised it (`from`: a named reviewer, code scanning), and `listed` (true: `open` only because the stage listed it, with no check of its own on it). The line is at most 8,000 characters: the findings that fit are named in order, and `more` counts the rest. The stage lists each open finding first as `open` (`listed`), and the finding check (`plugin/scripts/lib/finding-check.mjs`) writes each outcome from the branch it took and whether the finding ended resolved, never from a reply's or a note's words:

| Outcome | When |
| --- | --- |
| `fixed-test` | The test the answer named fails without the fix and passes with it, and the finding is resolved |
| `fixed-verdict` | The decision model judged it `fixed` at the confidence floor, and the finding is resolved |
| `answered` | The decision model judged it `rebutted` or `decided` at the floor (resolved without a change), and the finding is resolved |
| `not-fixed` | The named test failed on the head, or any other verdict (below the floor, `unresolved`, `decided` where a decision does not settle it) |
| `not-checkable` | No verdict (the decider failed), or no decision model and no test that decides: the answer stands unjudged |
| `open` | No answer of Pendulum's in that stage, or its resolution could not be posted |

Resolved means the evidence went up and the thread was resolved, or the thread was already resolved when the check reached it, in which case nothing more is posted: a `fixed-*` or `answered` outcome may have no evidence post in its thread.

The report reads these lines only from Pendulum's own review progress comments (Trust, above): a findings line anywhere else, or in anyone else's comment, counts nothing. A finding's outcome is the one the latest stage that names it recorded, in a record written (the comment's last edit) before `--until`, so the same period reads the same however late it is run; a later stage's `listed` `open` replaces only an earlier `open`, never a judgement; with no record written in time it is `open`. It was raised by the stage whose record names the review it came from, under that stage's model (the review stage's own: its reviewer's), or, for a reviewer outside Pendulum, by that login, in the stage that first listed it. A finding of Pendulum's reviewer whose review no record names is under `key` null: a review no recorded stage posted, such as one made in a session (`/pendulum:pendulum-review-here`) or by a stage from before the record. A finding counts when the stage that raised it (else the one that first listed it) started in the period.

- `totals` and each Objective's `findings`: `findings`, `outcomes` (a count per outcome code), `reviewStages` (the review stages in the period with a findings record), `cost` (their dollars, as the stage's cost counts them: every pass, the reviewer's, the answer pass and the fix passes, and the decision calls its usage record books, the finding check's among them, a `decision` row in the `pass` breakdown; a recorded stage the cost join has no part for is one run of unknown cost, never $0) and `unnamed` (the findings their records count but do not name). Decision calls made outside any stage are under `breakdowns.stage` `decisions`, in no review stage. An Objective's `findings` is null when it has no findings record at all, and zero counts when its records are all outside the period.
- `byReviewer`: `{key, outside, findings, outcomes, reviewStages, cost}`, `key` the reviewer model, or the login of a reviewer outside Pendulum (`outside` true, its `reviewStages` and `cost` null: Pendulum pays it nothing). A model's row counts its recorded review stages and their dollars, those that raised no finding included.
- `byStage`: `{issue, stage, model, findings, outcomes, cost}` per recorded review stage in the period, `stage` its run key; `findings` the ones its own reviewer raised.
