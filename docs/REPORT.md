# The cost report

`pendulum report` reads what Pendulum's own records say about a period or a set of Objectives and prints every figure already computed: as JSON with `--json` (the schema below), else as plain Markdown for a terminal or CI. Pendulum is only the data layer. Your coding agent presents the JSON however you ask (a table, a chart, an HTML page, a document) through the [`/pendulum:pendulum-report`](../plugin/skills/pendulum-report/SKILL.md) skill, and it never adds, averages or derives a dollar figure itself.

```sh
pendulum report [--since DATE] [--until DATE] [--issue N[,N...] | --label NAME | --parent N | --compare A,B | --compare-label A --compare-label B] [--json] [--max-calls N] [--by account] [--rates MODEL[,MODEL...]] [--app ID] [--self LOGIN]... [--repo OWNER/NAME]
```

- **Scope.** `--since` and `--until` (a date, or a time with its zone; `--until` is exclusive) give a period: every Objective with a stage that started in it. `--issue`, `--label` and `--parent` (the parent issue's sub-issues) give a set of Objectives, read whole unless a period is given too. `--compare` and `--compare-label` give two sets, the arms of an experiment, side by side (Compare two arms, below). A period with no `--since` (and no set) starts 30 days before `--until`, or before now, at that day's UTC midnight (`scope.defaultPeriod`). A decision-spend line, which records one UTC day's decision calls, counts when that day's start (its midnight) falls in the period.
- **Reads.** The issue comments hold every marker the report needs: the progress, harness, spend, ledger, vendor, decision-spend and finish markers, and a review stage's findings record (below). For `--issue`, each named issue's own comments are read; otherwise one paged repository-wide read (`issues/comments?since=`). GitHub filters that read by when each comment was last updated, so for each Objective with a record in it whose issue was opened before the period's start, the report also reads the Objective's own thread, as `--issue` does, up to its first comment written since the start (every comment the repository-wide read lacks was written before it), each comment kept once: every figure comes from all of the Objective's records, so a period's figures, its review findings and sandbox included, add up with the next period's to those of both together. After the PRs, each Objective merged in the period whose issue was opened before its start, and whose thread is not read yet, has it read the same way, for its figures per merged Objective (below). The Objectives' issues and the PRs on their `pendulum/<n>` branches give each one's outcome (for `--issue`, each branch's own PRs). A period's PR list is read newest updated first and stops at its first page older than the period's start (or the set's oldest issue), so it holds every PR updated since then; a closed Objective whose issue is older than that, with no PR of its own branch created since then in the list, has its branch's own PRs read too. A merged Objective is read from its finish card's summary alone (below), its stage comments and usage artifacts unread, when the card is Pendulum's own, every stage of Pendulum's in it has its per-pass figures, its run (its clock to its merge) lies in the period, every stage comment of the Objective in the period is one the card counted, and no stage comment of the Objective, Pendulum's or one by someone who can push, was written or edited after the card (a late vendor booking, a changed spend line); its figures are then the same its stages give, and its review findings still come from its review stages' findings records, each stage's dollars from the card. With `--by account` or `--rates`, whose figures no summary carries (each stage's account; a Codex stage's sub-agent threads and long-context share), no Objective is read from its summary: its stages are read. `--by account` reads every stage instead: a stage's account is in its own records only. A stage's per-pass ledger, tokens, Pendulum release and (when its harness line names none, as a Copilot stage's at its default model whose event stream named none) model come from the ledger line beside its spend line (below), with no artifact read, even where its usage record is read for another stage of the Objective, as its finish card takes them: a stage's figures are the same whichever path reads them. A stage's model is the first of its own harness line's, its ledger line's and its usage record's that names one (a usage record marked `byok`, of a Copilot pass through a bring-your-own-key provider, names none: its id is the provider's own), as the finish card names it; never another stage's, and null (`no-model`) when none does. Usage artifacts are listed and read only for the stages whose comment does not say everything: a stage with no spend line, for its dollars; a stage with no ledger line (a stage from before it, or one whose ledger did not fit) whose ledger no local record in this clone holds, for those figures; and a stage whose harness line and ledger line name no model, for its model, unless a local record in this clone names it. A stage with a spend line keeps that line's dollars, so none is counted twice: its ledger, from its comment, else its usage record or local mode's own record of it in this clone, gives its passes and tokens, never its total. A usage record that names no stage comment, beside a comment of its stage with dollars (cost-join's `unmatched`), may be that stage: its dollars are unknown, and none of its passes or tokens is counted. An artifact read with no `result.json` in it (its stage ended before writing one) leaves its stage one run of unknown cost, `unreported`. A cloud sandbox stage that left no progress comment counts the model dollars its vendor comment carries (`modelUsd`); one with none, and no model run of unknown cost, is one run of unknown cost (`unreported`), as on its finish card. The Objectives with a stage that has no spend line are read first, so a call budget that runs out costs per-pass detail before a dollar figure. A record that names a stage comment the report does not attribute to Pendulum is that stage's: it adds no stage and no dollars. Artifacts uploaded before the period began are not read. An artifact a fork's run uploaded (its workflow run's head repository is not the repository), or one that names no repository id, is never read, whatever its name: it is counted as `artifact-foreign`. Each artifact read is downloaded with `gh run download`, which reads its run's artifact list, and kept by its artifact id under your cache directory (`$XDG_CACHE_HOME`, else `~/.cache`; `~/Library/Caches` on macOS), in `pendulum/artifacts/<API host>/<owner>/<name>/`, with only the fields the report reads, never in the repository, so a later report downloads it no more. Local mode's own records under `<clone>/.pendulum/<n>/` are read for the per-pass ledger when the command runs in the clone. `breakdowns.model` depends on the clone too: a stage's split by Claude Code's per-model usage, and by a ledger only its local record holds, is made only where that record is, so the same period read elsewhere books those stages by their ledger line or whole, by stage (Dollars by model, below). No job or list of workflow runs is read, and no timeline but those a labelled set needs (Who applied the label, below) and those an old finish card's clock needs: an Objective whose newest finish card does not record why a clock is not known (its summary has no `clockWhy`, a card from before it was recorded) and names a labelled start has its timeline read once, planned, to check whether it was labelled before the rename to Pendulum, as such a card's start may be too late; these are read last, after the usage artifacts, so a call budget that runs out costs a wall clock (`timeline-unread`) before a dollar figure; nothing is written.
- **Budget.** The command prints its planned GitHub API calls to stderr before it reads: a paged read's first page, then, from that page's `Link` header, the pages left (said as "at least" where GitHub names no last page), until a page whose `Link` header names no next page (a full first page with no `Link` header at all ends the read too, and `no-link` says so); a read that can end early (the PR list, newest first, at the first PR older than the comments read; an Objective's own thread at its first comment written since the period's start) plans each further page as it is reached; for `--issue`, each issue's comment pages from its comment count; each Objective's own thread from before the period's start, its first page; after the comments read (and after each Objective's own threads read), the permission of each organization member or collaborator whose comments with a marker it reads (in a set, those on its issues alone), all of a read's new ones at once (Trust, below); for a label whose applier is checked, each listed issue's timeline and, as each one is first met, a labeller's permission; each login's permission is read once per report, a labeller's and an author's alike; after an Objective's artifact lists, the downloads its artifacts not yet cached take, two each; last, after the artifacts, the timeline of each Objective whose finish card is from before it recorded why a clock is not known and names a labelled start. A merged Objective read from its summary plans no artifact read. It stops at `--max-calls` (300 by default). A stopped report still prints what was read, with `source.complete` false, and exits 3: an Objective whose PRs were not read (the list, or its own branch's) has no outcome (null) unless a state label or an open issue decides it, and a stage whose usage record was not reached is of unknown cost for `stopped`. An Objective whose comments were not all read (`comments-stopped`) is printed from those that were: the comments after them, when the repository-wide read or its own was cut short, are one run of unknown cost for `stopped`, and so is each vendor comment whose stage's progress comment was not read (it may book for a stage before the period).
- **Token.** Local mode's token for the repository when it is saved, else your `gh` login: the report shows only what that login can read.
- **Trust.** A marker counts only from Pendulum's own login (the token's), a login `--self` names (each one, exactly, and only on a comment by someone who can push), or Pendulum's App. The App's numeric id comes from `--app`, else the repository variable `PENDULUM_APP_ID` (one call; `pendulum setup` writes it beside the secret of that name; when GitHub answers the token 403 or 404, which a token without Variables: read may get, one more call with your `gh` login's token, never printed; that read failing, or refused, never ends the report), else its local copy beside the local token (`~/.config/pendulum/repos/OWNER/NAME.json`, which setup, `serve` and doctor save: LOCAL.md, Set it up), else `PENDULUM_LOCAL_APP_ID`. No name or slug ever stands in for it. With none, `source.missing` says why, whether or not a comment through an App was read: `app-unreadable` (no token may read the variable) or `app-not-given` (it is not set). Someone who can push is the repository's owner, or an organization member or collaborator whose permission on the repository, read once per login (one call each, planned), is write, maintain or admin, as the finish card counts them (a maintainer the workflow names, too: the card counts their records on GitHub's answer alone, as the report must, which cannot see the workflow's maintainers): the author association alone says only that a person belongs to the organization or was added to the repository, as a read-only member or a read- or triage-role collaborator has, so the comments with a marker of a member or collaborator who cannot push count nothing, a spend line of theirs no figure. A member or collaborator whose permission the call budget stopped the report before reading (`author-unchecked`), or that the token may not read (a 403 that is no rate limit, `author-unverifiable`; a rate limit ends the report with an error), is never trusted: their stage comments are stages of unknown cost (`unattributed`), never their figures, and their decision-spend lines decision calls of unknown cost, as for a writer who is not Pendulum, below. A stage comment by anyone else who can push, or through another GitHub App, is a stage of unknown cost (`unattributed`), never its figures; its vendor comment adds nothing to it, one with no progress comment is a run of unknown cost but no stage, and its decision-spend lines are decision calls of unknown cost. Anyone else's, `github-actions[bot]` included, counts nothing. A finish card's summary counts only on Pendulum's own comment, as every marker does; anyone else's card is no summary, and the Objective's stages are read. `source.trusted` lists the logins and the App the report trusted, and the `--self` logins it did not trust, with why. For an install from before the variable, save it once: `gh variable set PENDULUM_APP_ID --repo OWNER/NAME --body <the App's numeric id>` (doctor names the same line when it is missing, and on GitHub Actions when it names another App).
- **Before the rename.** A stage recorded before Clockgrove Factory became Pendulum has a progress comment headed `<!-- factory:<n>:progress:<work|review>:<key> -->`, and an issue labelled then has `factory:objective` or `factory:running` label events (GitHub keeps a renamed label's old name on its events). The report reads none of them: no dollars, tokens, passes or start of the clock.
  Each such comment written in the period, on its own issue, by anyone whose stage comment would count (Pendulum's own, someone who can push, or through another GitHub App), and on an Objective the scope covers, is listed under `legacy-records` in `source.missing`, with its Objectives and the stages among them. The report is then partial, `source.complete` false, but exits 0, since no wider budget reads them. Each Objective row counts them in `legacyStages`, so an Objective with stages from both sides of the rename is marked.

## Compare two arms

Tag the Objectives of each arm of an experiment (a model, an effort, a setting) with an experiment label, `pendulum:experiment:<name>` ([USAGE.md](USAGE.md), Run an experiment), then put the two arms side by side:

```sh
pendulum report --compare sonnet,opus          # pendulum:experiment:sonnet against pendulum:experiment:opus
pendulum report --compare-label wave-6 --compare-label wave-7   # any two labels
```

- **Arms.** `--compare` takes two experiment names (the part after `pendulum:experiment:`), joined by a comma or as the flag twice; `--compare-label` takes two label names. Each arm is every issue with its label, read whole: `--since` and `--until` are refused with them, since a period would cut a merged Objective's dollars to the stages in it. An issue both arms count for is in neither, listed in `comparison.inBoth`.
- **Who applied the label.** An arm's label counts for an issue only when the person who last applied it, by the issue's timeline, can push or is a maintainer the workflow names, as for the harness and model labels: a label applied by anyone else, by a bot or through an App is not counted (`untrusted`, missing code `label-untrusted`), nor one whose labeller's permission the token may not read (a 403: `unverifiable`, missing code `label-unverifiable`). An issue both arms list counts only once each arm's check of it ended. A `--label` that is an experiment label follows the same rule. The check reads each listed issue's timeline once, and each labeller's permission once, before any other record of it; the other labels a `--label` names are read as GitHub has them.
- **Per arm:** n (its Objectives) and their outcomes; then, over its merged Objectives, the median and p25..p75 of the dollars per merged Objective of each billing kind, the model minutes (every harness pass of each stage's per-pass ledger, from its progress comment's ledger line or its usage record; for a merged Objective read from its finish card, the summary's `modelMinutes`, as are its fix passes and its wall clock, `passes.fix` and `labelledWallMs`, the same figures), the wall clock (from the Objective's start to the merge, the scorer's rule: its first `pendulum:objective` or `pendulum:running` label, or for one filed as a draft the start that led to its first Running), the review rounds (`reviewRounds`, below; for a merged Objective read from its finish card, its summary's stages marked `noPass`, or whose spend line says so, are none: a card from before `noPass` was on its summary falls back to the spend line) and the fix passes; and its runs of unknown cost with their reasons. A kind's median counts every measured merged Objective, so one billed at another kind counts as $0 in it. A merged Objective with a run of unknown cost is left out of every dollar measure, and one whose measure was not recorded out of that measure (model minutes and fix passes: a stage with no ledger, or a stage comment Pendulum cannot attribute to itself; wall clock: no start event, or a label event from before the rename, on its stages and on its finish card alike, whenever the card was written: Before the rename, above); each is counted as `leftOut`. A merged Objective with stages from before the rename to Pendulum (`legacyStages`) has figures that leave those stages out, so it is left out of every measure, counted as `leftOut` and listed in the arm's `legacy`; an Objective of the arm with such stages is left out of its review findings too. The medians and quartiles interpolate between the closest ranks, the same computation as the bench's baseline line.
- **No claims.** The figures stand alone: no difference between the arms, no test of significance, no total across kinds or across arms. Every measure carries its n, and the Markdown shows each arm's rows under the table. The Markdown shows the comparison and the source only; the JSON's `totals`, `breakdowns`, `objectives` and `hotSpots` cover both arms together.

## Rate ratios

`--rates` names candidate models (`--rates gpt-6.1-terra,gpt-6-luna`, or the flag again; an alias and the model it names count once). For each recorded model, the report gives each candidate's published rates over that model's own, on that model's recorded token mix, as one ratio with no unit:

- **One vendor only.** A ratio is read from one vendor's published rate table, the same for both models, and two vendors' models are never compared (`other-vendor`). Pendulum holds one table, OpenAI's (the Codex price table, `plugin/scripts/lib/codex-prices.json`), whose rows are the candidates it can weigh. Anthropic's models have none, since Claude Code reports its own dollars, so a Claude model gets no ratio (`no-rate-table`), as the recorded model or as a candidate. A candidate that is the recorded model is said (`same-model`), not weighed.
- **The mix.** Each model's tokens over its stages: a stage's tokens (as `totals.tokens` counts them) under its model, except that a Codex stage's sub-agent threads (its usage record's `subAgents`) are under each thread's own model and the rest under the stage's, as the cost join prices them. A stage's decision records (its ledger's `decision` entries) are in no model's mix. A stage whose ledger's passes ran on more than one model (a review stage's review pass on the reviewer's model, its answer and fix passes on the worker's), or on another model than the one its harness line names (an alias or a dated snapshot of it aside), is split by its passes: each model gets its own passes' tokens, and a Codex sub-agent thread its record lists comes out of the passes it ran in and goes under its own model. A pass's long-context share is known from a ledger only when its whole input is under the threshold. A stage that may have run passes on a model other than its own with no ledger naming each pass's model (a review stage with no ledger, or whose ledger line does not name a pass's model) gives its model and the worker's no ratio (`pass-models`). Where no record splits the sub-agent threads out (a stage known only from its progress comment, or from another harness's record), the passes on the worker's model cannot be split from the Objective's sub-agent model when that is another: both models get no ratio (`sub-agents`); a review pass on the reviewer's model marks neither. A model with a stage that recorded no input or output tokens gets none (`no-tokens`).
- **The weighing.** As the cost join prices Codex tokens: the uncached input (input less its cache reads and writes) at the input rate, the cache reads at the cached rate, the cache writes at the write rate (the input rate for a model with none) and the output at the output rate; and the long-context share (the tokens of requests over the table's `longContextAbove`, from each stage's usage record) at each model's long-context rates. Two models with no long-context rates weigh every token at the standard rates. Otherwise a share not known (a stage whose input passed the threshold with no usage record saying how much of it came from requests over it: its requests not read, or its tokens from its progress comment alone) gives no ratio, nor does a share that is not zero where either model has no long-context rates (`long-context`). A promotional rate counts only before its end (`price-ended` after it).
- **No dollars.** A ratio is not a cost and has no dollar unit; the report holds no figure of what another model would have cost. The `/pendulum:pendulum-report` skill's proposals, written only when the user asks, cite these ratios as given.

## Rules

- Dollars are vendor-reported (Claude Code's `total_cost_usd`, Copilot's AI credits, the AI Gateway's cost, the sandbox vendor's usage at its list rates), or API-reported tokens at the published rate (the Codex price table), or unknown. Nothing is estimated, bounded or counterfactual. The figures are the scorer's own: both use one cost join (`plugin/scripts/lib/cost-join.mjs`), each stage counted once across its usage record and its spend line.
- Totals stay split by kind and are never added together: `api` (billed per use on a key), `subscription` (API-equivalent at list prices, on the subscription), `credits` (Copilot AI credits, billed to the seat), `unknown` (billing not recorded) and `sandbox` (the cloud sandbox vendor's cost). The report carries no total across kinds.
- Spend that was not reported is a count of runs of unknown cost, each with its reasons, never $0. A run is a harness pass, a stage with no record of its own, a decision call, a sandbox vendor's unreported usage (a vendor comment with no stage of its own among them), or what the call budget left unread (`stopped`: a stage's usage record, or an Objective's comments after those read); `unknownDecisionCalls` says how many of a cost's runs are decision calls, and `unknownUnsplitRuns` how many no record says are decision calls or not. A spend line written while its stage ran (`running`) names how many of its runs were still open then (`usdRunning`): while the Objective's state label is `pendulum:running` and the line is on its newest stage comment, those runs are in flight, reason `running`, counted but not read as runs that ended unreported (when every run of the line is open, its own reasons give way to `running`); a stage killed after the write and started again has a later stage comment, so its line is read as it stands, as it is once the label is gone. Like `outcome`, the reason depends on when the report is run: the same runs read `running` while the Objective runs and `unreported` after.
- Hot spots are ranked by exact counts and ratios of them; nothing is flagged against a threshold.
- No reason, Block text, task card, transcript or credential string is in the report, and no account unless `--by account` asks for it.

## Schema

The JSON's `schema` is `pendulum-report/2`. A change that renames, removes or retypes a field, or changes what it measures, is a new version; a new field is not. Dollars are US dollars, rounded to a millionth. A **cost** is `{usd: {api, subscription, credits, unknown, sandbox}, unknownRuns, unknownDecisionCalls, unknownUnsplitRuns, unknownWhy}`: the known dollars of each kind (0 for none), the runs of unknown cost, the decision calls among those runs, the runs among them no record says are decision calls or not, and their reason codes. A stage's decision calls of unknown cost are its per-pass ledger's decision records' runs of unknown cost; with no ledger, its spend line's `usdByPass.decision` runs of unknown cost; with neither, all its runs when every reason it gives is a decision call's (`decision`, `decision-*`), else every run is unsplit, never counted as no decision call. Each run of Pendulum's own decision-spend lines is one decision call; a comment Pendulum cannot attribute to itself, a stage's or a decision-spend line's, gives no figure, so its run is unsplit. A **pass** is a harness pass (a work, review, answer, fix or conflict pass of the per-pass ledger); a decision record (a stage's decision calls, one ledger entry) is no pass, and is counted apart as `decisionRecords`. **Model minutes** are the minutes from a harness pass's start to its end; a decision record's times (its first call to its last) are none. **Tokens** are `{input, output, cached, written}`, summed where a record reports them (input includes the cache reads and writes), null where none does.

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `schema` | `pendulum-report/2` |
| `repository` | `OWNER/NAME` |
| `generatedAt` | When the report was made (ISO) |
| `pendulum` | The Pendulum release that made it |
| `scope` | `since`, `until` (ISO or null), `defaultPeriod`, `issues`, `label`, `parent`, `compare` (the two arms' label names, or null), `by` (the extra breakdowns asked for; with `account`, no Objective is read from its summary) |
| `words` | The words for each cost kind, to label the figures with exactly |
| `totals` | `objectives`, `stages`, `passes` (the harness passes of the per-pass ledgers), `decisionRecords` (their decision records), `cost`, `tokens`; `asked` and `churn`, the sums of the Objectives' rows of the same names (`{what, usd, unknownRuns}` and `{row, stages, usd, unknownRuns}`, in the finish card's order); `perMerged` (Per merged Objective, below) |
| `unknown` | `runs`; `decisionCalls` (the decision calls among them) and `unsplitRuns` (those no record says are decision calls or not); `stages` (the stages with runs of unknown cost: a decision record, or a vendor comment with no stage of its own, is an item but no stage); `reasons`: `{code, stages, runs, text}` per reason code, `stages` the stages (and decision records) naming it and `runs` its runs where no stage gives it with another reason, else null; `items`: `{issue, stage, runs, decisionCalls, unsplitRuns, why}` per stage with unknown cost (`stage` null for the comments of an Objective the call budget left unread) |
| `breakdowns` | `stage` (`work`, `rebase`, `review`, `decisions`: the decision calls outside a stage; null: an Objective's comments the call budget left unread), `pass` (by pass label: the ledger label without its count, `work-2` as `work`; from the ledgers; `decision` the decision records' row, whose `passes` is 0), `model` (one name per model: an OpenAI model the Codex price table lists by the table's id, after any provider prefix and through the table's aliases, a dated snapshot suffix kept, so `openai/gpt-6.1-sol` is `gpt-6.1-sol` and `gpt-5.6` is `gpt-5.6-sol`; a Claude model's other name that `plugin/scripts/lib/claude-models.json` lists (a GitHub Copilot catalog id) by the Anthropic API id it maps it to, so `claude-sonnet-5.5` is `claude-sonnet-5-5`; any other id as recorded; by the model each pass ran on, below), `harness`, `version` (the Pendulum release that ran the stage), `route` (the route each stage ran on, as its spend line records it, `route`: `actions`, `local` or `sandbox`, the stage's progress comment's line, else its vendor comment's, which is always `sandbox`; every line a stage writes records it, the lines written while it runs too, so a stage killed or stopped before its end has it; `decisions` for the decision calls outside a stage; null for a stage whose line records none (a line written before Pendulum recorded it, or no spend line: `route-unrecorded`), a stage Pendulum cannot attribute, a record that names no stage and a run the call budget stopped before; never inferred from who posted a comment), `day` (the UTC date each stage started, its progress comment's, else its vendor comment's, else its record's start; each comment's decision-spend lines of one day summed and rounded once; each decision-spend line's own day for the decision calls outside a stage; null for a comment of decision lines Pendulum cannot attribute and a run the call budget stopped before; oldest first, null last; `stages` counts stages alone). A `decisions` row of `route` counts one stage per comment of decision-spend lines, as `stage` and the other breakdowns do; `day` counts none for them. An Objective read from its finish card's summary alone, its stage comments gone, has its stages under a null `day` and `route`: those come from the stage comments, which the summary does not carry), and `account` with `--by account`. Each row: `key` (null where no record names it), `stages`, `cost`, `tokens`. A `pass` row has `passes` (its harness passes) and `decisionRecords` in place of `stages`, and `modelMinutes`: the model minutes of its harness passes that have their times, null for none (the `decision` row's). A `model` row's split of its runs of unknown cost follows its parts: a decision record booked by pass brings its decision calls with it, and the rest of the stage's split stays with what is booked by stage. A `model` row also has `basis`: `pass` and `stage`, each `{stages, cost, tokens}` (null where none was booked so), the row split by how its figures were booked; and `usdPerModelMinute`, as an Objective's (below), over the priced harness passes that ran on the model, each pass under the model its ledger names, as its dollars are booked (a stage's passes under its own model where they are booked by stage; a pass's whole dollars under the model the pass ran on, so in a Claude stage whose `cost` books its sub-agents under their own models, a pass's sub-agents' dollars count toward its own model's ratio). Every breakdown covers the totals' dollars and runs of unknown cost. `pass` has a row per pass label from the ledgers, with `modelMinutes` and `spendLineStages`: a stage with no ledger whose spend line names its dollars by pass kind (`usdByPass`: `work`, `review`, `answer`, `fix`, `conflict`, `decision`) puts those dollars under that kind, with no pass counted, its runs of unknown cost with its own reasons, and counts in that row's `spendLineStages`; then one row with `key`, `passes` and `decisionRecords` null, the dollars no pass names (decision calls outside a stage, stages with no passes known, and what a stage's figure holds beyond its passes': its cloud sandbox, runs of unknown cost), present when any is not zero, whose `tokens` are those of the stages with no ledger. It is never negative: a stage whose passes name more dollars of a kind, or more runs of unknown cost, than its own figure (`ledger-overruns`) keeps its passes, tokens and minutes counted in their pass rows, and only its dollars and runs of unknown cost move from them to this row, the stage's whole figure |

## Where the dollars went

Each Objective's `asked` and `churn` are its finish card's breakdown ([USAGE.md](USAGE.md#the-finish-card)) of its stages in the report, by the card's own rules (`plugin/scripts/lib/finish-card.mjs` `markChurn`, `spendBreakdown`), the same on both paths: each stage's dollars, runs of unknown cost and dollars by billing are its report figures; its dollars by pass kind (`usdByPass`), what it ran again after a Block (`rerun`) and whether it only judged answers (`judgeOnly`) come from its spend line, and a conflict rebase from its rebase line, in its own progress comment. A stage with no spend line stays whole under its own kind, its usage record's dollars by pass not read, so a merged Objective reads the same from its card's summary as from its stages.

- `asked`: the work the issue asked for, `{what, usd, unknownRuns}`, `what` one of `work`, `review`, `answer`, `fix`, `decision` (decision calls inside a stage), and `sandbox` for the cloud sandbox's row (its vendor's dollars under `sandbox`; a vendor comment Pendulum cannot attribute, of no stage, is a run of unknown cost in it). `usd` is each kind's dollars, those no billing names as `unknown`. A cloud sandbox stage that left only its vendor comment puts its model's dollars under its own kind (`work` or `review`), and its vendor's alone under `sandbox`.
- `churn`: the churn Pendulum caused, `{row, stages, usd, unknownRuns}`, `row` one of `rebase-conflict`, `rebase-clean`, `catch-up`, `judge`, `block`, with how many stages it came from.
- The rows add up to the Objective's stage dollars as the card's rows add up to its total; decision calls outside a stage, and runs the call budget stopped before, are in neither. The rows name no reasons for their runs of unknown cost: those are each stage's (`unknown.items`).

## Per merged Objective

`totals.perMerged` measures the Objectives merged in the period, as `--compare` measures an arm's (the median and p25..p75, interpolated between the closest ranks). Merged in the period: its outcome is `merged` and its merge (its row's `merged`: its finish card's, else its newest PR's) is at or after `--since` and before `--until`; with no period, every merged Objective of the set. `--until` bounds the merge, not the stages: each counts with every stage's figures, the period aside, a stage before `--since` or after `--until` included. Its whole thread is read for them: one opened before the period's start whose comments since then hold no record of it (its stages all before the period) has its thread read up to the period's start too, planned as the other thread reads are; then its figures come from the same reads, from its finish card's summary where its whole run is on it. So an Objective's figures here may exceed its row's, which the period cuts, and an Objective merged in the period may have no row at all.

`n` (the Objectives merged in the period); `usd` (per cost kind, over those with no run of unknown cost), `reviewRounds` (its review rounds, as an Objective's `reviewRounds`), `fixPasses` (over those whose ledgers count them, as an Objective's `passes`) and `wallMinutes` (over those whose row has one). Each is `{median, p25, p75, n, leftOut}`; a kind's median counts every measured Objective, so one billed at another kind counts as $0 in it. `leftOut` names each Objective left out of any measure, `{issue, why}`, `why` its codes: `comments-stopped` (the call budget stopped before its comments were all read: no measure; every other Objective merged in the period has its thread read whole), `legacy-records` (it has stages recorded before the rename to Pendulum, unread: its figures are a lower bound, so no measure, as an arm leaves it out), `no-records` (its thread holds no record of cost: no progress or vendor comment of a stage, no decision-spend line and no finish card summary, only, say, a plan from a work stage that died before its progress comment landed; or its figures hold no stage, dollar, run of unknown cost or decision record: no measure, never $0), `unknown-runs` (a run of unknown cost, such as a stage before the period whose usage record was not read: no dollars), `no-passes` (no fix passes), `no-clock` (no wall clock; its row's `clockWhy` says why).

## The finish card's summary

Each finish card ([USAGE.md](USAGE.md#the-finish-card)) carries one hidden line under its `<!-- pendulum:<n>:finish -->` marker: `<!-- pendulum:<n>:summary {...} -->`, its JSON on one line with `<` and `>` escaped. It holds the figures the card shows and each stage's per-pass figures, from the same records the card is drawn from, never worked out another way: a stage's passes come from its ledger (the ledger line beside its spend line, else its usage record's, its times to the millisecond) through the same code the report folds a stage's ledger with. `v` is its version, `1`: a change that renames, removes or retypes a field is a new version, and a reader reads the Objective's stages for a version it does not take, or for a summary any figure of which does not read. Dollars are US dollars to a millionth, `cents` the whole cents the card shows (its rows add up to the total's), times are ISO, and `ms` and `wallMs` are milliseconds. It is read only from the line under the `finish` marker, only from a card with at least one stage, and only from a merged card: the card of an end other than a merge (Blocked, a Question, closed without merging) writes `merged` null and `end` (`blocked`, `question`, `closed`) with `ended`, its time, and the report reads that Objective's stages. A card is written again in place as the run ends again; its figures are those of its last write, so a stage comment written or edited after that write sends the report to the stages. A line over 30,000 characters is written with every stage's `passes` null; still over it, or with the comment over GitHub's 65,536 characters, the card is posted without a summary.

## The per-pass ledger

Each stage's usage record (`result.json`: the GitHub Actions artifact `pendulum-usage-<n>[-review]`, or local mode's `<clone>/.pendulum/<n>/usage-*/`) carries `ledger`, one entry per harness pass and decision record: `label`, `decision` and `calls` for a decision record, `model`, `effort`, `started`, `ended`, `usd`, `usdUnknown`, `usdUnknownWhy`, `billing`, `usdByBilling` (its dollars by kind, a relayed key's share as `api`), `workerKeyUsd`, `tokens`, `resumed` and `firstCache` (its first request's cache split). The record's `usd`, `usdByBilling` and `usdByPass` are sums of its passes' dollars, each pass's to a millionth as its ledger entry has it, so the stage's dollars equal its passes' added up, to the millionth; only a relayed key's spend outside every pass (in `workerKeyUsd`) is in `usd` and in no pass. Its `ran` names the worker's and the reviewer's effort and the Pendulum release (`pendulum`). The artifact keeps it 90 days, and the report's cache keeps what it read.

Every stage, on every route, also writes its ledger into its progress comment, on the line after its spend line, when the stage ends: `<!-- pendulum:ledger {...} -->`, numbers and labels only (no reason, Block text, prompt, path or credential). Its keys are short: `v` (the Pendulum release), `m` (the stage's model), `b` (the first pass's billing) and `p`, one entry per pass with `l` (label), `d` (a decision record's calls), `m` and `b` where the pass's differ from the stage's, `e` (effort), `ms` (start and end, in Unix milliseconds; a line written before them has `t`, in Unix seconds), `u` (known dollars, to a millionth, as the spend line), `q` and `w` (runs of unknown cost and their codes), `y` (dollars by billing, where they are not all at the pass's own kind), `k` (a relayed key's share), `n` (input, output, cached and written tokens), `r` (1 when it resumed a session) and `c` (its first request's cache read and write). A model is written only when the stage's harness line already names it (its own, or the Objective's worker, sub-agent or reviewer model), so an id the harness resolved itself (a Bedrock inference profile ARN names an AWS account) never reaches the comment: a pass on another model reads as `m: null`. A stage whose harness line names no model at all (Copilot at its default, its event stream naming none) writes its own model from its usage record, a name from GitHub's model catalog. A ledger is not written when its line would pass 6,000 characters, or when it would take the comment past 60,000 (GitHub refuses one over 65,536): the comment says so in a line of its own, the marker carries the pass count alone (`{"cut": N}`), and the usage record stays that stage's ledger. The report reads the line for a stage's passes, tokens (its passes' summed), model and release, and the finish card for its model; both only on Pendulum's own comments (Trust, above), and neither for dollars: a stage's dollars are always its spend line's. A stage with no ledger line (one whose ledger did not fit, or from before the line) takes its ledger from its usage record or local record. Pass times are to the millisecond from either, and to the second from a line with `t`, on the stage path and the finish card alike.

### Dollars by model

`breakdowns.model` books a stage's dollars, runs of unknown cost and tokens by the model each pass ran on (`basis.pass`), wherever the stage's per-pass ledger names it: a review stage's review pass under the reviewer's model, its answer and fix passes under the worker's. The ledger is the one both the stage path and the finish card's path read: the stage's ledger line, else local mode's own record of it in this clone. A Claude stage whose local record holds Claude Code's own per-model usage (`models`, from its result's `modelUsage`, sub-agents included) is booked by that usage instead, so the sub-agents' model gets its own row; only when that usage accounts for exactly the ledger's harness passes (each harness pass's dollars reported, all of one billing kind, the models' dollars adding up to those passes' to the millionth each is rounded to), else by its passes. Its decision records are none of them, since Claude Code's usage never holds the decision calls: each keeps its own booking, under the model its ledger names, else under the stage's model, by stage. A model name from a local record, in its ledger or its per-model usage, counts only where the stage publishes it, as its ledger line would (its harness line's models: its own and the Objective's worker, sub-agent and reviewer): a name the harness resolved itself (a Bedrock inference profile ARN, which names an AWS account, or an alias such as `opus`) is never a row; per-model usage with one is not used, and a pass on one stays under the stage's model, by stage. What no pass names (a pass whose model the ledger does not name, the sandbox vendor's cost, dollars outside every pass) stays under the stage's own model, by stage (`basis.stage`), its runs of unknown cost with the stage's reasons no pass gives. A stage with no such ledger (none, or one only its usage artifact holds, which the finish card's path does not read), or whose passes add up to more than its own dollars, runs of unknown cost or tokens, is booked whole under its model, by stage. The split is exact, in millionths of a dollar: the passes' dollars, each rounded to a millionth on its own, may pass the stage's by that rounding, and that comes off the stage's own model's part. A stage counts once, under its own model, whatever models its passes ran on: a review stage whose passes all ran on other models is under the reviewer's model with `basis.pass` at $0 and one stage. So a row's `stages` count the stages whose own model it is, and its dollars may be other stages' passes: dollars over a row's stages are no cost per stage. The decision calls outside a stage are under `decisions`, by stage.

## Review findings

Each review stage records how every finding it handled ended, in its own progress comment's findings line (`<!-- pendulum:findings {...} -->`): `pr`, `reviews` (the ids of the reviews its own reviewer posted) and `items`, one per finding, `{id, outcome, review, from, listed}`: the finding's comment id (or `r<review>-<n>` for one in a review body), its outcome, the review that raised it (Pendulum's reviewer's), the login of the reviewer outside Pendulum that raised it (`from`: a named reviewer, code scanning), and `listed` (true: `open` only because the stage listed it, with no check of its own on it). The line is at most 8,000 characters: the findings that fit are named in order, and `more` counts the rest. The stage lists each open finding first as `open` (`listed`), and the finding check (`plugin/scripts/lib/finding-check.mjs`) writes each outcome from the branch it took and whether the finding ended resolved, never from a reply's or a note's words:

| Outcome | When |
| --- | --- |
| `fixed-test` | The test the answer named fails without the fix and passes with it, and the finding is resolved |
| `fixed-verdict` | The decision model judged it `fixed` at the confidence floor, and the finding is resolved |
| `answered` | The decision model judged it `rebutted` or `decided` at the floor (resolved without a change), and the finding is resolved |
| `not-fixed` | The named test failed on the head, or any other verdict (below the floor, `unresolved`, `decided` where a decision does not settle it) |
| `not-checkable` | No verdict (the decider failed), or no decision model and no test that decides: the answer stands unjudged |
| `open` | No answer of Pendulum's in that stage, or its resolution could not be posted |

Resolved means the evidence went up and the thread was resolved, or the thread was already resolved when the check reached it, in which case nothing more is posted: a `fixed-*` or `answered` outcome may have no evidence post in its thread.

The report reads these lines only from Pendulum's own review progress comments (Trust, above): a findings line anywhere else, or in anyone else's comment, counts nothing. A finding's outcome is the one the latest stage that names it recorded, in a record written (the comment's last edit) before `--until`, so the same period reads the same however late it is run; a later stage's `listed` `open` replaces only an earlier `open`, never a judgement; with no record written in time it is `open`. It was raised by the stage whose record names the review it came from, under that stage's model (the review stage's own: its reviewer's), or, for a reviewer outside Pendulum, by that login, in the stage that first listed it. A finding of Pendulum's reviewer whose review no record names is under `key` null: a review no recorded stage posted, such as one made in a session (`/pendulum:pendulum-review-here`) or by a stage from before the record. A finding counts when the stage that raised it (else the one that first listed it) started in the period.

- `totals` and each Objective's `findings`: `findings`, `outcomes` (a count per outcome code), `reviewStages` (the review stages in the period with a findings record), `cost` (their dollars, as the stage's cost counts them: every pass, the reviewer's, the answer pass and the fix passes, and the decision calls its usage record books, the finding check's among them, a `decision` row in the `pass` breakdown; a recorded stage the cost join has no part for is one run of unknown cost, never $0) and `unnamed` (the findings their records count but do not name). Decision calls made outside any stage are under `breakdowns.stage` `decisions`, in no review stage. An Objective's `findings` is null when it has no findings record at all, and zero counts when its records are all outside the period.
- `byReviewer`: `{key, outside, findings, outcomes, reviewStages, cost}`, `key` the reviewer model, or the login of a reviewer outside Pendulum (`outside` true, its `reviewStages` and `cost` null: Pendulum pays it nothing). A model's row counts its recorded review stages and their dollars, those that raised no finding included.
- `byStage`: `{issue, stage, model, findings, outcomes, cost}` per recorded review stage in the period, `stage` its run key; `findings` the ones its own reviewer raised.
